## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
**1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution**
FarmDirect is a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers and bulk buyers. Farmers can upload their products, set prices and receive orders through the platform. It also provides AI-based price suggestions, demand forecasting and delivery support.
**2 How It Addresses the Problem**
- Reduces the number of middlemen.
- Helps farmers earn better profits.
- Provides agricultural products at reasonable prices.
- Reduces food wastage through demand prediction.
- Reduces transportation costs using route optimization.
**3 Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution**
- AI-based product price recommendations.
- Future demand prediction for agricultural products.
- Direct farmer-to-consumer connection.
- Smart delivery route optimization.
- Regional language support for easy farmer access.

## Technical Approach
Farmers register and upload their agricultural products.
Buyers browse products and place orders
AI suggests suitable prices and predicts product demand.
The system connects farmers with transport providers.
Products are delivered directly to buyers.

## Feasibility and Viability
**Feasibility of the Idea**
FarmDirect is easy to develop using existing web technologies and AI tools. Farmers and buyers can access the platform through smartphones or computers.
** Potential Challenges and Risks**
- Lack of digital knowledge among some farmers.
- Poor internet connectivity in rural areas.
- Changes in agricultural product prices.
- Transportation and delivery difficulties.
  ** Strategies to Overcome Challenges**
- Provide a simple, user-friendly interface.
- Include regional language support.
- Update market prices regularly.
- Connect with reliable transport providers.

## Impact and Benefits
**Impact on the Target Audience**
- Helps farmers sell products directly to buyers.
- Provides consumers with fresh products at reasonable prices.
- Supports small-scale farmers and local businesses.
- Improves agricultural market accessibility.
** Benefits of the Solution**
Social Benefits:
- Supports rural development.
- Encourages digital awareness among farmers.
Economic Benefits:
- Increases farmers' earnings.
- Reduces unnecessary middlemen costs.
Environmental Benefits:
- Reduces food wastage through demand forecasting.
- Saves fuel through optimized delivery routes.
